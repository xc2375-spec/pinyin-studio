04Spelling patterns
When do the dots of ü disappear?＋

nǚ · lǜ: dots stay. jù · qù · xué · yú: dots disappear, but the sound stays ü.

Three abbreviated finals＋

-iu = iou · -ui = uei · -un = uen

y and w are spelling, not new sounds＋

i → yi/y- · u → wu/w- · ü → yu
